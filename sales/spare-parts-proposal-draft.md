## 2. Our recommendation: launch B2B in Pune first

| Phase | Who buys | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Pune B2B** | Garages and parts shops | They already know part numbers, reorder every week, place larger orders, and you can sign them up in person. |
| Phase 2: Vehicle owners (B2C) | Individual car and bike owners | Needs a reliable "fits my vehicle" catalogue and marketing spend. Add it once Phase 1 has sellers and order volume. |
| Phase 3: Multi-city | Same, new cities | City-wise sellers, riders and pricing. |

Launching every buyer type in every city at once is the most common reason marketplaces run out of money. Phase 1 proves that garages will reorder through the app, and that is the proof investors and sellers want to see.

## 3. Phase 1 scope

**Buyer app (Android + web) for garages and parts shops**
- Sign-up with shop details and GST number (optional), approved by admin
- Search by part name, part number, brand, and vehicle make/model (as tagged by sellers)
- Compare the same part across sellers by price and availability
- Cart, order, and pay by UPI/card (Razorpay) or cash on delivery
- Order status updates, order history, one-tap reorder, invoice download

**Seller panel (web) for wholesalers and distributors**
- Bulk catalogue upload via Excel, plus stock and price updates
- Accept or reject orders and mark them ready for pickup
- Sales and commission statements, payout report

**Rider app (Android)**
- Assigned pickups and drops, navigation link, status updates
- OTP-based proof of delivery, COD collection record

**Admin panel**
- Seller, buyer and rider onboarding and approval
- Commission rules by seller or category
- Live order board, rider assignment, cancellations and returns
- Settlement and GST-ready reports

**Not in Phase 1** (quoted separately when needed): the vehicle-owner app (B2C), iOS apps, a full vehicle-fitment database, credit/pay-later for garages, live GPS tracking on a map, and multi-city setup.
