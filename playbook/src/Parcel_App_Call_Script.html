If he says

"Can you do lower?""I can do ₹70,000 if we start today." Move once, never below ₹70k.
"Everything is available with AI.""Yes, we use AI too. That's why it's ₹75k and not ₹2 lakh. You're paying for me to make sure it all works: payments, tracking, OTP, store approval."
"Let me think.""What's the one thing stopping you?" Solve it, or fix the next call date now.

Don't

  • Compare with other developers
  • Promise a store launch date. Apple and Google decide review time
  • Promise discounts on his other ideas. "Let's do this one well first."
  • Add features on the call. "Noted, I'll price it as phase 2."
  • Take legal responsibility. Terms are his to get checked

After the call

Paid: agreement today, kickoff tomorrow, update the tracker.
Not paid: send the proposal PDF on WhatsApp with the next call time.
