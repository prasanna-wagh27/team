The six steps to the advance.

What to do at each step, and how you know it's done. Prices and customer types are on the Sales Sheet.

1Chat
Where
The same channel they came from. DM stays in DM, WhatsApp stays on WhatsApp.
Do
Ask Q1 and Q2 from the Sales Sheet. Once you know what they want, share the price range from the price list.
Done when: you know their customer type, what they want, and they're fine with the price range.
2Call
Where
Phone or WhatsApp call.
Find out
Running business: how it works today, the biggest headache, volume (orders, bookings, team size), who decides, when they want it live.
New idea: what they've done so far, their capital, who their first users are.
End with
Fix the Google Meet: day and time.
Done when: you can write the proposal and the Meet is booked.
3Proposal
Do
Start from the proposal template. Prices come from the price list. Give 3 plans where possible. If the scope is above ₹4L, split it into phases.
Send
Before the Meet as a preview, always with the Meet time already fixed. Never as a standalone attachment.
Done when: they have the proposal and the Meet is confirmed.
4Google Meet
Order
1. Repeat back their need in their words
2. Show the demo app on screen
3. Walk through the proposal, page by page
4. Answer their questions
5. Ask which plan fits them
Done when: they've picked a plan, or you know exactly what's stopping them.
5Close
If yes
Send the UPI link and agreement the same day, while you're still on the Meet if possible.
If not yet
Fix the next Meet date before you hang up. Follow up only with the serious ones.
Done when: the advance is paid, or the next Meet is booked.
6Advance and kickoff
Do
40% by UPI link, then the agreement, then a kickoff call within 24 hours. After that, a progress update every week.
Done when: money is in the bank and the kickoff date is fixed.

2 · European booking marketplace
Screenshots or a short screen recording of the dashboards. Don't name the client.
"I built a multi-vendor booking platform for a company in Europe: vendors, bookings, payments, dashboards."
3 · Our own loyalty platform
Screenshots of the merchant dashboard and customer view.
"We built and run our own loyalty platform. The points system in your app comes from it."
Keep ready in one folder on your phone: the demo APK, the admin panel link, the booking platform recording, and the loyalty screenshots.
