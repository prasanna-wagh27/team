04 · PRICING MODEL

Two ways to set prices. You choose.

Decide this before we start. It changes how the vendor app and vendor payouts work.

SIMPLER TO START
OPTION A · YOU SET THE PRICE
One price list for all of [City]

You set every selling price. Each vendor is paid the purchase price you agreed with them. You keep the difference.

Same price in every zone
Easier for vendors, faster to launch
Full control of offers
You agree purchase prices with each vendor
Your team updates prices when the market moves
OPTION B · VENDORS SET THE PRICE
Each vendor runs their own catalogue

Each supermarket sets its own products and prices. You take a commission on every order from their zone.

Less price work for your team
Vendors can run their own offers
Prices can differ from zone to zone
Each vendor must keep their catalogue updated
Takes longer to set up
Example: ₹500 order
Option A: vendor paid the agreed ₹460, you keep ₹40
Option B: 8% commission, you keep ₹40, vendor gets ₹460

Numbers are only an example. In both options you also keep the delivery fee you set.
