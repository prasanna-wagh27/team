02 · WHAT'S INSIDE

One app, two modes, one admin panel.

Anyone can send and carry from the same app. Switch between Send and Carry with one tap.

SendApp
  • Post a parcel: route, date, size, photo, price
  • See offers from carriers
  • Accept or counter-offer
  • Chat with the carrier
  • Live tracking on the map
  • Pickup and delivery OTP
  • Parcel history and ratings
CarryApp
  • Go active on a route: from, to, date
  • See parcels on the route within a radius
  • Accept or make an offer
  • Chat with the sender
  • Directions to pickup and drop
  • Location shared during the trip
  • Earnings history and ratings
Admin panelWeb
  • All users and carriers
  • ID checks: approve or reject carriers
  • Every parcel and its status
  • Commission settings
  • Payments and transactions
  • Complaints, block users
  • Dashboard: parcels, users, earnings

04 · HOW YOU EARN

Three ways to take your commission.

Let's pick one together. In every option, numbers stay hidden and chat stays in the app until your commission is paid, so deals don't move to WhatsApp.

WE RECOMMEND
OPTION A
Sender pays a booking fee

When a carrier accepts, the sender pays your commission online. The carrier gets the agreed price directly at delivery.

Carriers join free, so you get more of them
You're paid before the trip
Carrier's money is cash or UPI outside the app
OPTION B
Carrier wallet

Carriers top up a wallet by UPI. Your commission is deducted each time they accept a parcel.

Nothing extra for senders to pay
Commission is automatic
Carriers pay before they earn
OPTION C · LATER
Full payment in the app

Sender pays the full amount in the app. The carrier is paid after delivery OTP, minus your commission.

Most trust, no cash
Needs carrier bank details and payouts
More setup: priced separately
Included in the price: option A or option B. You set the commission (a % or a fixed amount) from the admin panel.
