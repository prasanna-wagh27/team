How an order works

1
Customer opens your app, picks products and pays by UPI, card or cash on delivery.
2
The order appears instantly on your admin panel. Your staff packs it.
3
A rider gets the order on the delivery app, picks it up and heads out.
4
Customer gives the rider a delivery OTP. Done, and the sale shows in your reports.
You decide how delivery works, from the admin panel, any time: instant delivery or time slots, delivery fee, minimum order, free delivery above an amount, and delivery area.

Honest note: Blinkit's 10-minute delivery runs on dedicated warehouses and riders on standby. With one store and your own riders, plan for fast local delivery, usually 20–40 minutes depending on distance and riders.
