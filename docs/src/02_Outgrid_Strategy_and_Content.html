Every proposal: three tiers. Launch ₹1.5L (core) · Growth ₹2.5L (tracking, wallet, offers, analytics) · Scale ₹4L (full custom). Most owners pick Growth. Pushback moves them down a tier; the same scope never gets cheaper. Year-one value of one client ≈ ₹1.5L build + ₹12k care × 10 + ₹6k automation × 10 = ₹3.3L.
Pricing check: price ≥ freelancer cost × 2. Freelancers cost ~₹30k/month each. The multiplier covers sales and project management, rework, slippage, the free-fixes month and profit. Example: 2 freelancers × 2 months = ₹1.2L cost → at least ₹2.4L, priced ₹2.5–3L. Below 2×, cut scope.

Risk reversal (in every proposal): fixed price in writing · 40/30/30 as you see progress · if we miss the launch date because of us, two months of care free · code, domain, store accounts and data in the client's name · weekly WhatsApp video update · 30 days of free fixes. Third-party costs (servers, SMS, maps, gateway, Google Play $25 once, Apple $99/year) are paid by the client.

6Maximum 2 builds a month, delivered with freelancers.Quality and launch dates are our only reputation.

Part B · Content · 6 · Direction

From "start a business" to "make more money in yours".

Tarun Udyojak keeps its identity: founder stories and business strategy. What changes is the five posts a week that carry an Outgrid call-to-action: they speak to owners who already earn, and they let the build be the proof.

TrackSharePurposeFormats

C · Filtered opportunity15%Keep founder reach, filtered.Clone-app topics with "not a franchise" and "from ₹1.5 lakh" on screen.

How every post turns into a lead

  1. Each reel ends with a keyword CTA: "Comment APP" (or HISHOB, ORDER).
  2. Everyone who comments gets a DM with S1 (price range + "what's your business and city?"). Manual is fine; automate later.
  3. Score the reply on the five checks. 4–5 checks → call today.
  4. In the tracker, note which reel the lead came from. Measure reels by qualified leads, not views.

Weekly rhythm

MonA · commission or payback mathsThuB · demo build or behind the scenes
TueB · screen walkthroughFriC · filtered opportunity
WedA · segment pain (distributors, supermarkets, garages)DailyStories: polls, DM replies (names hidden), build clips

Production: one 2-hour shoot covers the week

  • Saturday morning: shoot five reels back to back. Face to camera for hooks; screen recordings for demos.
  • 30–45 seconds each. The hook on screen in the first 2 seconds. Marathi speech, Marathi + English subtitles.
  • Numbers on screen are written as "उदाहरण" (example) unless they're a real owner's, shared with permission.
  • Never present a demo as a client, or an example as a real story. Our credibility is the page's credibility.
