2 · Qualify in two messages

Price in the first reply protects your time.

A 6.5 lakh audience sends many messages; few can pay ₹1.5 lakh. Say the range early. People who can't pay leave quietly; people who can feel respected.

4–5 checks: book the call today. 3: one more WhatsApp exchange. Under 3: polite exit, broadcast list, no call time. Score every lead 0–5 in the tracker.

2 · Qualify (continued)

Reply bank: save these as WhatsApp Business quick replies.

Always add their name and one detail from their message, so it never feels copy-pasted.

S1 · Any inbound

नमस्कार! आम्ही businesses साठी delivery, ordering आणि marketplace apps बनवतो: customer app, rider app आणि admin panel. असे projects साधारण ₹1.5 ते 3 लाखांमध्ये होतात. तुमचा business कोणता आणि कोणत्या शहरात आहे?

We build delivery, ordering and marketplace apps for businesses. These projects usually land between ₹1.5 and 3 lakh. What's your business, and which city?

S2 · "Franchise?"

एक गोष्ट clear करतो: ही franchise नाही. App, brand आणि business पूर्णपणे तुमचा. आम्ही technology बनवतो, आणि projects ₹1.5 लाखांपासून सुरू होतात. Business आधीपासून चालू असेल किंवा गुंतवणुकीची तयारी असेल, तर नक्की बोलूया.

This isn't a franchise. The app, brand and business are fully yours; projects start at ₹1.5 lakh. If the business is running or capital is ready, let's talk.

"Ready-made app?"

Ready-made template आम्ही विकत नाही, कारण त्यात code आणि control तुमच्याकडे राहत नाही. आम्ही तुमच्या business साठी app बनवतो, आणि ते 100% तुमचं असतं. तुमचा business सध्या चालू आहे का?

"Free demo?"

नक्की. Demo आम्ही तुमच्याच नावाने आणि products ने बनवतो. त्यासाठी आधी 15 मिनिटांचा call करूया, म्हणजे demo तुमच्या business ला खरंच लागू पडेल. उद्या 4 वाजता चालेल का?

Only after 3+ checks: a branded demo costs you 3–4 hours.

Budget too low

खरं सांगतो: ₹__ मध्ये customer, rider आणि admin app असलेलं custom app शक्य नाही, आणि मी खोटं promise करणार नाही. पुढे budget तयार झाला की नक्की msg करा.

Idea stage, no capital

तुमची idea छान आहे, पण आत्ता ती सुरुवातीच्या टप्प्यात आहे असं वाटतं. Business plan आणि capital तयार झाले की नक्की बोलूया.

"Equity देतो"

Equity मी फक्त paid build सोबत घेतो, payment ऐवजी नाही. तुमचा विश्वास असेल तर ₹1.5L चा Launch tier हा कमी risk चा start आहे.

Voice notes: reply with a short Marathi voice note (under 40 seconds) and type the price line, so the number is in writing.

Four listening tools

Tell me more: "याबद्दल अजून सांगा." Use it on every complaint.
Mirror: repeat their last 2–3 words as a question.
Number it: "How many a week? What's one worth?"
Summarise: "So __, __ and __. Did I get that right?" The first yes of the sale.

Payback maths (use their numbers)

Restaurant: ₹4L/month on aggregators × ~25% ≈ ₹1L leaving. Move 30% of orders to own app ≈ ₹30k/month saved. ₹1.5L app ≈ 5 months payback.
Distributor: ₹50L/month sales; 3% more repeat orders ≈ ₹1.5L more sales; at ~10% margin ≈ ₹15k/month profit plus staff hours.
Round down. If payback is over 12 months, say so and suggest Launch.

Developer traps: explaining the tech stack · agreeing to every feature (ask "what does it save or earn?", park the rest for Phase 2) · filling silence · quoting a price to "close" the call. After the call: tracker updated, confirmation WhatsApp within an hour, self-score on the scorecard (section 8).

5 · Demo and proposal walkthrough

Show their app. Then walk the plan live.

We have no testimonials yet. The branded demo replaces them: once an owner sees their own name and products on a working app, the question becomes "when", not "whether".

The white-label base (build once)

  • Customer/buyer app (PWA is fine for demos): browse, search, cart, pay screen, status.
  • Admin panel: live order board, products, customers, daily sales.
  • Rider or seller view: the order visibly travelling.
  • Brand config: name, logo, colours, catalogue from CSV.
  • Realistic seed data: Indian names, Pune/Nashik areas, real prices.
  • Variants: distributor/marketplace first (serves spare parts and agri), then restaurant, then booking.

Branded demo checklist

  • Only for 4–5 check leads with a walkthrough booked.
  • Logo and 10–20 products from their Instagram, Google listing or catalogue.
  • One feature that answers their leak (credit limits, no-commission checkout).
  • Tested on a phone over mobile data.
  • Under 4 hours. If longer, improve the base.

The 2-minute video, sent with S6

0:00–0:10Their logo on the home screen: "__ ji, this is your app."
0:10–0:50A customer orders and pays in four taps.
0:50–1:20The order pops up on the admin board: "no WhatsApp scrolling".
1:20–1:45Rider or seller accepts and delivers.
1:45–2:00The fix for their leak, then "see you at [time]".

6 · Close and collect

Ask for the money. Then stop talking.

First-time founders rarely lose on price; they lose by never clearly asking. The owner is leaking money every month. Asking them to start is helping them stop it.

STEP 1 · Choice

तुम्हाला कोणता option योग्य वाटतो: Launch की Growth?

STEP 2 · S7 · The ask

काम सुरू करण्यासाठी 40% advance, म्हणजे ₹__. मी महिन्याला फक्त 2 projects घेतो आणि पुढचा slot __ पासून आहे. Payment link आत्ताच पाठवतो; payment आलं की आजच agreement आणि kickoff ची तारीख पाठवतो.

STEP 3 · Silence Say nothing. The first person to speak after the price usually gives something away. Send the payment link while they're still on the call.
S8 · "Start later"

जानेवारीत सुरू करायचं असेल तर ₹25,000 देऊन slot आत्ताच book करू शकता. ही रक्कम advance मधून वजा होते, आणि आजची किंमत lock होते.

S9 · Follow-ups

Day 2: proposal बद्दल काही प्रश्न आहेत का? या आठवड्यात सुरू केलं तर __ पर्यंत launch होऊ शकतं. · Day 5: __ पासूनचा slot अजून मोकळा आहे. Hold करून ठेवू का? · Day 10: सध्या योग्य वेळ नसेल हे समजू शकतो. तयार असाल तेव्हा फक्त START लिहा.

6 · Close and collect (continued)

Twenty objections. Answer each aloud in under 15 seconds.

Acknowledge, ask one question, answer only that. Never argue.
