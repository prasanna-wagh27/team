Show their app. Then walk the plan.

We have no testimonials yet. The branded demo replaces them: when an owner sees their own name, logo and products on a working app, the question changes from "can they do it?" to "when can we start?"

Time: 45 minutes read + record the base demo + mock walkthrough with the CEOPairs with playbook sections 06 and 07
01 · The base

One white-label base. Three minutes to rebrand.

Build once, this weekend. Every branded demo after that is configuration: name, logo, colours, 10–20 products. If a demo takes more than 4 hours, the base isn't flexible enough yet.

Base must haveWhy
Customer/buyer app (PWA is fine for demos)Browse, search, cart, order, pay screen, order status. Owners judge by this screen.
Admin panelLive order board, products, customers, a daily sales number. Owners fall in love here.
Rider or seller viewShows the order travelling: the "it actually works" moment.
Config file for brandName, logo, colours, currency, sample catalogue from a CSV.
Seed data that looks realMarathi/Indian names, real Pune/Nashik areas, sensible prices. "Test Product 1" kills the magic.

Variants of the same base: restaurant (menu + rider), distributor (retailer ordering + credit + schemes), marketplace (multi-seller + commission), booking (slots instead of cart). Build the distributor/marketplace variant first: it serves spare parts and agri.

Branded demo checklist (per qualified lead)

  • Only for leads with 4–5 checks and a walkthrough slot already booked.
  • Logo, colours and 10–20 products pulled from their Instagram, Google listing, Zomato page or catalogue.
  • Use their real outlet name, area and product names. Prices close to real.
  • One feature that answers the leak they told you about: credit limits for the distributor, the "no commission" checkout for the restaurant.
  • Tested on a phone, in mobile data, not just on your laptop.

02 · The walkthrough call (15 minutes)

Never send a PDF and wait. Share your screen and walk it.

MinuteDoSay
0–2Recap their goal and leak, in their words."Last time you said ₹__ a month goes to __. Today's plan fixes that."
2–5Open the demo live. Let them tap around if possible."Try placing an order yourself."
5–9Three options side by side."Most owners like you pick Growth. Here's why."
9–11Timeline with milestones and the weekly demo."You see progress every week before each payment."
11–13Price, 40/30/30, care plan, risk reversal."The number in the proposal is the number you pay."
13–15The ask (Module 04)."Which option feels right for you?" Then stop talking.

Presenting the three options

  • Describe them right to left: start with Scale (₹4L) so the full vision is on the table, then Growth, then Launch. Growth now looks sensible, and Launch is the safe floor.
  • Recommend one. "If I were in your place, I'd take Growth, because [their leak]." Owners want an expert opinion, not a menu.
  • Show what's excluded, not just what's included. It prevents fights later and signals experience.
  • Tie every tier to money: "Launch stops the order errors. Growth adds tracking and offers, which bring repeat orders."

Proposal: the 8 parts (from the playbook)

  1. Their goal in their words + the payback maths.
  2. 3–4 screens from their branded demo.
  3. Three options side by side, with inclusions and exclusions.
  4. Timeline with 3–4 milestones (two weeks of buffer built in).
  5. Price, 40/30/30 and the care plan.
  6. Risk reversal: the six promises.
  7. Proof: the European booking platform and your loyalty SaaS, with dashboard screenshots.
  8. Valid for 7 days, plus the next available start date.
