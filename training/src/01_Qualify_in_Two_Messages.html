Qualify in two messages.

A 6.5 lakh audience sends you many messages. Most can never pay ₹1.5 lakh. This module teaches you to find the few who can, in two WhatsApp replies, without being rude and without spending a call.

Time: 45 minutes read + practise on today's real inboxPairs with playbook sections 05 and 08 (S1, S2)
01 · The idea

Your time is the scarcest thing in the company. Price protects it.

The old way: every "details?" got a friendly chat, then a call, then a quote that shocked them. The new way: the price range comes in the first or second reply. People who can't pay leave quietly. People who can pay feel respected, because you were straight with them.

Old reply (costs you an evening)

"Hello sir! Yes we make all types of apps. What features do you need? Let's connect on call 😊"

Result: a 40-minute call, then "₹2 lakh? I was thinking ₹30,000."

New reply (costs you 30 seconds)

"Hi! We build delivery, ordering and marketplace apps for businesses: customer app, rider app and admin panel. These projects usually land between ₹1.5 and 3 lakh. What's your business, and which city?"

Result: only people who can afford it answer the question.

4–5 checks: book the call today. 3: one more WhatsApp exchange. Under 3: polite exit and add to the broadcast list. No call time.

Green flags and red flags

Green: move fast

  • Mentions a shop, outlets, staff, retailers or a monthly number.
  • Complains about Zomato/Swiggy commission, WhatsApp order chaos or unpaid credit.
  • Asks "how long will it take?" before "how much?"
  • Has a GST number, a website, a Google listing or an existing customer base.
  • Replies in full sentences, at business hours.

Red: exit politely

  • "Franchise", "dealership", "how much can I earn?"
  • "App like Zomato for my village" with no business and no capital.
  • "Send me the free demo first" before telling you anything.
  • "I'll give you equity / partnership instead of payment."
  • "My budget is ₹20–30k for the full app."
02 · Reply bank

The first messages you'll get, and what to send back.

Save these as WhatsApp Business quick replies. Always add their name and one detail from their message, so it never feels copy-pasted.

"Details?" / "Price?" Use S1 from the playbook: what we build + ₹1.5–3L + "What's your business, and which city?"
"Franchise आहे का?" Use S2: "not a franchise, the app and business are fully yours, projects start at ₹1.5L, let's talk if the business is running or capital is ready."
"Ready-made app आहे का?"

Ready-made template आम्ही विकत नाही, कारण त्यात code आणि control तुमच्याकडे राहत नाही. आम्ही तुमच्या business साठी app बनवतो, आणि ते 100% तुमचं असतं. तुमचा business सध्या चालू आहे का?

We don't sell ready-made templates, because then the code and control aren't yours. We build the app for your business and it's 100% yours. Is your business running today?

"Free demo पाठवा"

नक्की. Demo आम्ही तुमच्याच नावाने आणि products ने बनवतो. त्यासाठी आधी 15 मिनिटांचा call करूया, म्हणजे demo तुमच्या business ला खरंच लागू पडेल. उद्या 4 वाजता चालेल का?

Sure. We build the demo with your own name and products. Let's do a 15-minute call first so the demo actually fits your business. Does 4 pm tomorrow work?

Only offer this after they pass at least 3 checks. A branded demo costs you 3–4 hours.

Budget too low

खरं सांगतो: ₹__ मध्ये customer, rider आणि admin app असलेलं custom app शक्य नाही, आणि मी खोटं promise करणार नाही. पुढे budget तयार झाला की नक्की msg करा.

Honestly: a custom app with customer, rider and admin apps isn't possible at ₹__, and I won't make a false promise. Message me whenever the budget is ready.

Idea stage, no capital

तुमची idea छान आहे. आत्ता ती सुरुवातीच्या टप्प्यात आहे असं वाटतं. Business plan आणि capital तयार झाले की नक्की बोलूया. तोपर्यंत Tarun Udyojak वरचे founder stories बघत राहा.

Your idea is good. It looks early-stage right now. Once the business plan and capital are ready, let's definitely talk. Until then, keep following the founder stories on Tarun Udyojak.

"Equity देतो"

Equity मी फक्त paid build सोबत घेतो, payment ऐवजी नाही. तुमचा विश्वास असेल तर ₹1.5L चा Launch tier हा कमी risk चा start आहे.

I only take equity on top of a paid build, not instead of payment. If you believe in it, the ₹1.5L Launch tier is the low-risk start.

Voice notes: many owners send voice notes. Reply with a short voice note in Marathi (under 40 seconds) plus the price line typed out, so the number is in writing.
