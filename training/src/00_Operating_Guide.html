02 · Reporting

The nightly report: five lines, every night, even when it's all zeros.

COPYReport · [date]

1. Messages sent: S5 __ · outbound __ · follow-ups __
2. Replies: __ (qualified 4–5 checks: __)
3. Calls: booked __ · held __ · walk-ins __
4. Demos sent __ · proposals walked through __
5. ₹ collected: __
Stuck on: [one line] · Tomorrow's top 3: [three lines]

What I do with it: compare it against the funnel, tell you which deal to push, which to drop, and rewrite any script that isn't getting replies. If the same step stalls two nights running, we change the method, not the effort.

The Sunday review (30 minutes)

  1. Update the real funnel numbers: conversations → qualified → calls → demos → proposals → advances.
  2. Find the leakiest step. That is next week's only training focus.
  3. Kill any deal with no reply after the day 10 follow-up (S9). Move it to the broadcast list.
  4. Pick the content track that produced the most qualified leads and double it.
  5. This is the only time strategy can be reopened. Write any change into DECISIONS.md.
03 · Decision protocol

How to stop overthinking.

Overthinking is an open decision that keeps running in the background. The fix is to close decisions fast and in writing, not to think harder.

SituationWhat you do
Covered by the playbook or DECISIONS.mdDo what it says. No debate.
Not covered, easy to undo (wording, which lead first, a time slot)Decide within 10 minutes. Write it in the report. I'll correct it if it's wrong.
Not covered, hard to undo (a price, a discount, a scope promise, a start date, a contract term)Say "Let me confirm and get back to you by tonight." Message me. Never commit on the spot.
New idea (ready-made apps, ads, a new segment, a new service)Write it in a "parking lot" line in the report. We look at it on Sunday, not before.
Feeling stuck or unsureDo the next small physical action: send one message, make one call. Action is the cure; more planning is not.
Three sentences to say to yourself: "Done beats perfect." · "A no today is better than a maybe for a month." · "My job today is conversations, not certainty."

Daily practice (15 minutes, before the 3:30 calls block)

  • Say the advance ask (S7) out loud three times. Your voice must not drop or rush on the number.
  • Five random objection cards from Module 04, answered aloud.
  • Re-read your notes from yesterday's calls: what did the owner say about money?

The non-negotiables

  • No work starts before the advance is in the bank. Not a screen, not a spec.
  • No price below ₹1L for a build. No discount for the same scope.
  • No proposal sent as a PDF and left alone. Always walked through live.
  • No deal without a next step and a date in the tracker.
  • No promise you can't keep with freelancers: launch dates carry two weeks of buffer.
  • No talking down the competition. Ask the three questions (rider/admin apps, code ownership, Saturday-night support) and let the owner judge.
