Question sequence (pick in this order)

  1. Why him: "What's your background in auto parts? How did you spot this gap?"
  2. Supply: "How many wholesalers have agreed to list? Where does their stock data live today: Tally, Busy, Excel?"
  3. Demand: "Who are your first 50 garages? How will you get them to order through the app instead of calling their usual shop?"
  4. Fitment: "How will a garage know a part fits? Part number, vehicle model, or the seller confirms?"
  5. Speed: "What delivery time are you promising garages? Two hours? Same day?"
  6. Money: "Commission %? Credit or prepaid for garages? Who pays for delivery?"
  7. Returns: "Who absorbs the cost of a wrong-part return?"
  8. Decision: "Capital set aside for tech? Who else decides? When do you want the first order to go through?"

If he can't answer 2 and 3, he isn't ready for a ₹3L build. Offer the Launch tier, or ask him to come back once 5 sellers have agreed.

02 · Steering the scope

He'll want everything. You sell Phase 1.

SAY THIS

B2B, B2C आणि pan-India एकदम सुरू करणं हे marketplaces चे पैसे संपण्याचं सगळ्यात मोठं कारण आहे. आधी Pune मध्ये garages आणि parts shops साठी B2B सुरू करू. Garages ना part numbers माहीत असतात, ते रोज order करतात, आणि त्यांना तुम्ही स्वतः भेटून onboard करू शकता. ते चाललं की investors आणि sellers दोघांनाही proof मिळतो.

Starting B2B, B2C and pan-India at once is the biggest reason marketplaces run out of money. Let's start B2B in Pune for garages and parts shops. Garages know part numbers, order daily, and you can onboard them in person. Once that works, both investors and sellers have proof.
