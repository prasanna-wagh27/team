The discovery call.

You are a doctor, not a salesman. You diagnose where the business is losing money, show the owner the number, and only then prescribe. Thirty minutes, and you talk 30% of the time.

Time: 1 hour read + role-play with the CEO (pass mark 16/20)Pairs with playbook section 06
01 · Mindset

Developers lose discovery calls in one specific way.

You know how to build everything, so the instinct is to explain features: "we'll use Flutter, real-time tracking, admin dashboard…". The owner hears noise. What they want to hear is that you understood their business and their money.

Developer instinctDo this instead
Explain the tech stack.Ask how orders come in today.
Agree to every feature they mention.Ask "what does that feature save or earn you?" and park the ones with no answer for Phase 2.
Fill silence with talking.Count to three. The owner fills it with the real problem.
Quote a price at the end to "close" the call.Promise a branded demo in 48 hours and book the walkthrough slot.
Accept "it's for my town" as the business case.Ask "who will be your first 100 customers, and how will they hear about it?"

The 30-minute structure

BlockGoalYour key line
Open · 2 minSet the agenda and permission to be honest."I'll understand your business, show you where an app makes or saves money, and tell you honestly if it doesn't. Okay?"
Today · 8 minHow the business actually runs: orders, volume, people, tools."Walk me through yesterday: from the first order to the last delivery."
The leak · 8 minFind the cost in rupees, hours or lost customers."What does that cost you in a month?"
Payback · 5 minDo the maths live, with their numbers."So roughly ₹__ a month is leaking. Let's see how long an app takes to pay back."
Decision · 4 minBudget, decision-maker, launch date."Who else will be part of the decision? When do you want to launch?"
Next step · 3 minBook the walkthrough before you hang up."Give me 48 hours for a demo with your brand on it. Thursday, 7 or 8 pm?"
Opening in Marathi: "आज मी तुमचा business समजून घेईन, app मधून पैसे कुठे वाचतील किंवा वाढतील ते दाखवेन, आणि app ची गरज नसेल तर तसंही स्पष्ट सांगेन. चालेल?"

Four listening tools

Tell me more"याबद्दल अजून सांगा." The single most useful sentence in sales. Use it on every complaint.
MirrorRepeat their last 2–3 words as a question. Owner: "Retailers just don't pay on time." You: "Don't pay on time?" They will explain the real problem.
Put a number on itEvery pain gets a number: "Roughly how many a week? What is one of those worth?"
SummariseBefore payback maths: "So orders come on WhatsApp, about 8 a week go wrong, and ₹4 lakh is pending with retailers. Did I get that right?" A yes here is the first yes of the sale.

Payback maths, done live

Restaurant
₹4L/month on aggregators × ~25% commission and ads ≈ ₹1L/month leaving.
Move 30% of orders to your own app → ≈ ₹30k/month saved.
₹1.5L app ÷ ₹30k ≈ 5 months to pay back.
Distributor
₹50L/month sales. Faster reorders and reminders lift repeat orders 3% → ₹1.5L more sales a month.
At a ~10% margin, ≈ ₹15k/month more profit, plus staff hours saved.
₹2L app ≈ pays back within a year, often faster.

Always use their numbers, and round down. Owners trust conservative maths. If the payback is longer than 12 months, say so honestly and suggest the Launch tier, or say the app isn't worth it yet. That honesty is what makes the next owner trust you.
