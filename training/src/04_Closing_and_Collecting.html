Ask for the money. Then stop talking.

Most first-time founders don't lose deals on price. They lose them by never clearly asking. This module trains the ask, the 20 objections you'll hear, and the mechanics of getting the advance into the bank the same day.

Time: 1 hour read + daily 15-minute drill until the first advancePairs with playbook sections 04, 07 and scripts S7–S9
01 · Mindset

Asking for an advance is service, not pressure.

The owner came to you with a leak costing them money every month. Every week they delay, the leak continues. Asking them to start is helping them stop it. If you believe the payback maths you did on the call, asking is the honest thing to do.

What you might feel

  • "They'll think I'm greedy."
  • "Let them decide in their own time."
  • "If I push, I'll lose them."

What's actually true

  • Professionals ask for advances. Hesitation looks inexperienced.
  • Without a deadline, "later" becomes "never".
  • A clear ask gets a clear answer. A "no" lets you move on today.

The ask, word for word

STEP 1 · Choice question

तुम्हाला कोणता option योग्य वाटतो: Launch की Growth?

Which option feels right to you: Launch or Growth?

STEP 2 · The ask (S7)

छान. काम सुरू करण्यासाठी 40% advance, म्हणजे ₹__. मी महिन्याला फक्त 2 projects घेतो आणि पुढचा slot __ पासून आहे. Payment link आत्ताच पाठवतो, payment आलं की आजच agreement आणि kickoff ची तारीख पाठवतो.

Great. To start, we take a 40% advance: ₹__. I take only 2 projects a month and the next slot starts on __. I'll send the payment link now; once it's in, I'll send the agreement and kickoff date today.

STEP 3 · Silence Say nothing. Count to ten in your head if you need to. The first person to speak after the price usually gives something away. Let it be them.

02 · Objection cards

Twenty objections. Answer each aloud in under 15 seconds.

Rule for every objection: acknowledge, ask one question, answer only that. Never argue.

#They sayYou say
1"Too expensive.""Compared to the ₹__ a month going out today? It pays back in about __ months. If you want a smaller start, Launch covers the core."
2"Someone quoted ₹40–50k.""Ask them: do you get rider and admin apps, who owns the code, and who fixes it on a Saturday night? If yes to all three, take their offer."
3"I'll think about it.""Of course. What's the one thing you're unsure about: the price, the plan or the timing?"
4"Send details on WhatsApp.""I will. Can we take 10 minutes tomorrow so I can answer your questions directly? 4 or 6 pm?"
5"Let me ask my partner / family.""Sure. Let's do 15 minutes with them tomorrow, so they hear it first-hand."
6"My CA / son will decide.""Great, let's include them. What will they want to check: the cost, the tech or the contract?"
7"Start after New Year."Slot booking (S8): "₹25k now, adjusted against the advance, and today's price is locked."
8"Give me a discount.""I can do 5% for full payment upfront, or for a video testimonial. Otherwise Launch is the lower-cost start."
9"Have you built this exact app?""Not this exact one, but the same engine: vendors, catalogue, orders, payments, dashboards. You just saw it with your brand."
10"You're new. Any clients?""I built a booking marketplace for a European company alone, and my own loyalty SaaS. And you pay in milestones, only after you see each one working."
11"What if you disappear?""Code, domain, store accounts and data are in your name from day one. You see a demo every week before you pay the next part."
12"Do it for equity.""I only take equity on top of a paid build. If you believe in it, Launch is the low-risk start."
13"Pay after it's done.""Milestones protect both of us: 40% to start, 30% when you see the core working, 30% at launch."
14"Can you finish in 3 weeks?""I could promise it, but I'd be lying. __ weeks is honest, and if we miss it because of us, two months of care are free."
15"Add this feature too, same price.""Happy to add it in Phase 2. I'll note it in the proposal with a price now so there are no surprises."
16"Why monthly care charges?""Servers, updates, store policy changes and fixes. Without it, an app slowly breaks. You can stop it anytime."
17"Zomato/Swiggy will still be needed.""Yes, keep them for new customers. Your own app is for regulars, where you stop paying commission."
18"My retailers won't use an app.""Start with your top 30. The salesman places orders for the rest through the same app. Usage grows when schemes appear only in the app."
19"Need a GST invoice.""Of course. Every payment comes with an invoice." (Confirm GST registration status with your CA beforehand.)
20"Let me compare a few agencies.""Please do. Compare the three things: rider/admin apps, code ownership, support. The proposal is valid for 7 days, and the slot after that is __."
03 · Collecting

Get the advance into the bank the same day.

Before your first walkthrough, have these ready

  • A bank account in the business name (Outgrid Labs), with UPI.
  • A Razorpay payment link (or bank details as text) you can create in under a minute.
  • An invoice template with business name, address, invoice number, client details, milestone and amount. Check GST rules with a CA; this guide is not tax advice.
  • A one-page agreement (below), ready to fill in with the client's name and option.

The one-page agreement: what it must cover

ScopeThe chosen option and its inclusions; the exclusions list.
Milestones and payment40/30/30 with what "done" means for each; payment due within 3 days of the milestone demo.
Change requestsAnything outside scope is quoted in writing before work starts.
OwnershipCode and assets transfer to the client on full payment; store accounts and domain in the client's name from day one.
Support30 days of free bug fixes after launch, then the care plan if chosen.
Third-party costsServers, SMS, maps, payment gateway fees and store fees paid by the client.
TimelineLaunch date, plus the client's duties (content, approvals within 3 days) that the date depends on.

The same-day sequence

  1. On the call: the ask, silence, the answer.
  2. Still on the call: "I'm sending the payment link now." Send it while they're listening.
  3. Within the hour: agreement + invoice on WhatsApp and email.
  4. Within 24 hours of payment: kickoff call, timeline shared, first weekly update date fixed.
  5. That night: tell the CEO. Then line up freelancers for the kickoff date.

Follow-ups when they go quiet (S9)

Day 2: "Any questions? Start this week and you launch by __." · Day 5: "The slot from __ is still open. Shall I hold it?" · Day 10: "I'll close this here; type START when you're ready." Then stop. Polite persistence, never begging.

Pass mark: all 20 cards answered aloud in under 15 seconds each in a timed drill with the CEO, and the S7 ask said with the real number, without hesitation, three times in a row.
