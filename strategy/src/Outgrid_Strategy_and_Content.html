Decisions we made, and why.

Settled. Reopen only if real results say otherwise.

₹4L cap per project or phaseThat's what our clients can realistically spend. Bigger scope is split into phases. The delivery multi-store plan (₹4.5L) is the one exception.
Lower prices now, for resultsReal launched apps and demos matter more than margin in the first months.
No founding-client discountA discount signals inexperience and makes ₹3–4L harder to trust.
No ready-made "launch kit"It only has value if the buyer actively runs it. Most won't, and they'd blame us.
No agencies as partnersThey'd see how it's done and build it themselves with AI.
No ride appsLicence costs make them unviable for our clients.
Setup and app store launch includedFeels premium, costs us little. The client pays third-party fees directly.
Inbound only, for nowLeads come from the Outgrid Labs page. Meetings in person only in Pune or Mumbai. Paid ads start after the first project's money comes in.

Content: the rule.

Famous app in the hook. Aimed at owners. The famous name gets the reach. Talking to owners gets the right people. Avoid "start a business in your town"; that's what pulls dreamers.

By the number of DMs from customer types 1–5, not by views. Note it in the tracker for each reel.
